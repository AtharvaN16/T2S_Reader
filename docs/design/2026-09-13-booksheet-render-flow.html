The Book sheet's rendering flow

Render mode asks the reader to give up three things — listening progress, the ability to play a chapter, and their bookmarks — in exchange for a checkbox. That trade is the flow's real problem, and it has one cause: a single slot at the end of the row being asked two different questions.

One position, two questions, and you can only ever see one answer

The trap in the last line: allowsHitTesting(renderMark?.isSelectable ?? true) makes a ready chapter's row inert. The chapters most ready to play are the only ones you cannot tap. Render mode is where you go to find out what is playable, and it is the one place that will not play it.

02 / THE FLOW, STEP BY STEP

Where the reader is dropped

Open the sheet from Home or the Collection. Nothing on screen says any chapter is on the device.
⋯ → “Render chapters”. The list changes meaning under you: rings and checks become selection marks, bookmarks disappear. No heading says you have changed mode — only the summary line and the foot bar appear.
Tap rows to pick. A ready row does not respond, and nothing explains why.
“Start rendering (3)”. The selection clears, the bar reads “Done”. The three rows you just queued look identical to the rows you didn't until the queue reaches them one at a time.
Watch, or leave. Leaving is the cliff: close the mode and every mark goes. Reopening the sheet shows the normal list, which says nothing about rendering.
No way to stop. Not in the sheet, not anywhere — cancel() and cancelAll() are written and tested but no view calls them.
When the queue drains, a 4-second toast. If the sheet is open you also see the ticks appear. If it is not, that toast is the only notice you will ever get.
03 / THREE DIRECTIONS

Each one is a different answer to “should this be a mode at all?”

04 / THE RECOMMENDATION

D2, with D3's block as the header

They compose. D2 makes every row honest; D3's block gives the book a total and the two controls that have nowhere else to live. Together they remove the mode entirely and nothing is hidden at any moment.

  • The row — device state as words in the duration line; trailing slot stays listening-only; bookmark pill always present; every row always tappable to play.
  • The header block — “On this device · 612 MB · 6 of 14”, a progress bar while the queue runs, Choose chapters and Stop.
  • Actions — long-press a chapter: Play from here · Render this chapter · Select chapters… · Remove audio. The permanent trash leaves the list.
  • Selection — leading checkboxes, ready rows dimmed-but-explained, key says cost not count.
  • Evict — “Remove audio”, with the sentence about what it frees and what it keeps. Delete-the-book moves out of the ⋯ that holds it, so the reversible and the irreversible are never adjacent.

Cost: ChapterRenderMark loses its selection cases and becomes a small status enum; ChapterRow gains a status clause; BookSheet loses isRendering as a display mode and keeps it only for selection. The app-wide strip, Storage and badges stay deferred — this direction doesn't block any of them, and the header block is the piece the strip would later mirror.
