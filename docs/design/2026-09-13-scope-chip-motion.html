01

The status line, moved up

Today it is an overlay centred across the clock row, in ink2 — or glow while the voice warms. Moved above the chapter picker it lands on the tail of the text fade, where ink2 grey against dimmed body text is exactly the wrong colour: both are grey, and the one that matters is the smaller. Three ways to keep it readable.

Where the fade is. The ground gradient starts 64 pt above the bottom block and is solid by the chapter row's foot, so a line placed directly above the picker sits in the middle of the ramp — partly over body text, partly over ground, and the mix changes with scroll position. That is the argument for B: a line that carries its own ground does not care what is behind it.

02

The morph is already written

Press the bar in the mock below and watch what ThinScrubber does today: the chapter under the finger widens to activeShare = 0.45, rounds its corners, grows 6 pt → 12 pt, and the others dull to 0.5 — all on .spring(duration: 0.25). Chapter scope is that same layout with activeShare driven to 1.0.

What this buys. The scope switch is not a new animation, it is the existing one taken to its limit — so the two bars are visibly the same bar, and the press-to-scrub gesture and the chip end up speaking the same visual language. In code it is a single activeShare value moving between 0.45 and 1.0, with the segment loop, the layout maths and the spring all untouched.
The one thing that will pop. Ticks are laid out per segment from the book-wide 48. Chapter 2 owns about five of them at rest and needs forty at full width, so if the tick count changes when the scope does, the fill under the growing bar re-lays-out mid-spring and flickers. Fix: compute the current chapter's ticks at chapter resolution in both scopes and let them be squeezed while the bar is narrow. A tick grid that is too fine to resolve at 6 pt costs nothing; one that changes count mid-animation costs the whole effect.
03

Tune it

Both springs on one page so they can be judged together. The chip's thumb and the bar's morph should not run at the same speed — the chip is a small object that should feel snappy and settled before the bar has finished travelling, so the bar reads as a consequence of the tap rather than a co-event.

05

Notes for the build

  • ScrubberModel grows a scope. Ticks are a book-wide pass today; chapter resolution has to come from the same model, not from ThinScrubber slicing a coarse array.
  • Haptic. A soft .selection on the chip tap, nothing on the bar — the bar already fires on scrub.
  • The chip must not move the transport. A 22 pt pill sets the clock row's height; run the chip at 18 pt inside a row that keeps its current height, or the whole transport shifts down by six points the first time the bar is drawn.
  • Scrubbing in chapter scope. With activeShare at 1.0 the existing gesture already maps the finger to the current chapter — but the chapter picker above is then the only way to reach another chapter. Worth deciding whether a scrub to either end nudges into the neighbour.
  • Accessibility. accessibilityValue on the scrubber must name the scope — "42 percent of chapter 2" — because the same percentage now means two things.
