01 / YOUR PICK, DRAWN

What A + B looks like as one component

Both states, stacked, so the swap is visible.

The question under this: are these one component in two states, or two components? If they swap in place — and they do, on the same spot in the sheet — the eye expects the second to be the first with different words. A′+B′ is that. A+B is two boxes that take turns.

02 / THE WORD, AND THE BUTTON

“Evict” is a cache word. But “Delete” alone is a trap.

You are right that Evict has to go — no reader thinks of their audio as being evicted. The catch: this sheet's ⋯ already has a Delete that removes the book from the library for good. Two controls called Delete, one recoverable and one not, a few millimetres apart.

WordingReads asVerdict
Evict all todaySomething the system does to a cacheOut. Nobody's mental model.
DeleteDelete… what? The book is right there above itAmbiguous against the ⋯'s Delete. The one word that must not be ambiguous.
Delete audioThe audio goes, the book staysrecommended Says the noun. Pairs with the box's heading — “On this device · Delete audio”.
Delete downloadsFiles that came from somewhere elseWrong: nothing was downloaded. The phone made it.
Remove audioGentler, reversibleHonest — it is reversible — but the app says Delete elsewhere, and two verbs for one act is worse than one blunt one.

Whichever wins, move the book's own Delete. Right now ⋯ holds Bookmarks · Delete, and the box holds Delete audio. The reversible one and the permanent one should not share a screen without saying which is which — the permanent one should say “Delete book”, not “Delete”.

04 / WHAT THE EDGES DECIDED

Three rules the states forced

  • The segmented bar needs three states, not two. Done, part-done, none — because the fill tier leaves chapters half made and that audio takes up room. A plain bar cannot say this at all, which is why “Partly rendered” exists as a phrase today.
  • It falls back to a plain bar above ~30 chapters, and reads as one at 1. Same slot, same row, one view swapped.
  • The control changes with the state, and sometimes there is none: nothing rendered → no button; failed → Try again; no room → Stop only. A box whose bottom row is always “Delete audio” would be lying in three of the eleven states.

My recommendation: A′ + B′ reconciled, wording “Delete audio”, control placement D1, and the book's own Delete renamed “Delete book” in the ⋯.
