00 / THE AXES

What actually varies

Four decisions make all fourteen: what the hero is (megabytes, a fraction, a count, or nothing) · what shape progress takes (segments, a bar, a ring, chips, a waveform, none) · where the controls sit (a row of their own, trailing the detail, in the header, or hidden) · and whether it is a box at all.

15 / IF YOU WANT A STEER

Three worth trying on the phone

  • 01 Ledger — the safe one. Hero number, segments, controls trailing the detail.
  • 10 Folded into the heading — the interesting one. Costs no height at all, puts the bar directly over the list it measures, and removes a box from a sheet that already has a lot going on. Worth seeing before dismissing.
  • 08 Progress on the lip — the tidy one. Everything that is not the bar stays plain text, and the panel reads as a download tile, which is what it is.
