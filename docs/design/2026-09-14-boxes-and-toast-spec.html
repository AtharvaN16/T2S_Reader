01 / THE TWO BOXES

Why these two shapes are allowed to differ

Last round I argued they should match. Your split says otherwise, and it holds up: they are not two states of one thing, they are two different questions. Storage asks “how much of this book do I have?” — a quantity. Rendering asks “how far along is this?” — a rate. A quantity wants a hero number and a count you can see; a rate wants a heading and a bar.

What they do share, so they never look like strangers: the same 16 pt padding, the same ground well, the same 5–6 pt bar, and the same trailing control slot.

On icon-only buttons. Pause reads unambiguously as a glyph — every player in the world uses it. Stop does not. A bare ✕ next to a pause button reads as “dismiss this box”, not “throw away the queue”. Two ways out: give ✕ a confirmation (Stop rendering? 3 chapters queued.), or keep ✕ for dismiss-like weight and let Stop live in the row's accessibility label only. I would confirm it — it is the one control here that loses work.

05 / DECIDED HERE

Four things this spec settles

  • The two boxes may differ — a quantity and a rate are different questions. They share padding, well, bar weight and control slot, so they still read as siblings.
  • Delete is bare text, not a pill. On the storage box it is one word in destructive at the trailing edge; the confirmation sheet carries the weight.
  • Stop gets a confirmation. Icon-only is fine for pause; a bare ✕ that silently throws away a queue is not, and the sheet is where “finished chapters are kept” can be said.
  • The segmented bar falls back above ~30 chapters and at 1, and needs a third dim state for part-rendered chapters.
