Prepare on charge

It works, it picks the continue-book then the Queue, and the hours are hours of listening. None of that is on the screen. Four ways to put it there.

The order continue-document → Queue, each from its resume position, one shared budget
The unit playback seconds — "3 hours" is three hours of listening banked ahead of you
A / THE PROMISE, NOT THE SETTING

Lead with what you have, not with what to choose

The reader's question is "can I listen on the plane tomorrow", and the app knows the answer: 1h 32m. So say it first, at the size of an answer, and let the target be a quiet control underneath it.

B / INTENT, PRICED

Stop selling hours; sell the trip, and show the bill

"3 hours" is a number you have to translate. "A commute" is one you don't — and every option carries what it will actually take off the phone.

C / ONE SWITCH

Ask nothing. Let the app watch how much you listen.

The honest YAGNI reading: almost nobody knows whether they want 3 hours or 8, and the app has better evidence than they do — it knows the listening history and the cap.

D / THE QUEUE MADE VISIBLE

Answer "which document?" by showing the list, and draw the budget across it

This is the direction that matches the mechanism exactly: [lastPlayed] + queue, each from its resume position, one budget draining in order. That is a list with a line through it.
