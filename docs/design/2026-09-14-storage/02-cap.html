The cap

There is no cap on what the app takes. There is a cap on one of the four things it holds — the smallest of the two big ones — and the screen presents it as a total.

A / ONE BUDGET, WHOLE APP

Make the number the reader thinks they're setting the number they're setting

You pick a total. The stack above shows how it's spent. The audio cache is whatever the fixed parts leave — so lowering the budget squeezes the one band that can actually be squeezed, and tells you so.

B / SAME MECHANICS, HONEST LABELS

Keep the cap exactly as it is — stop it pretending to be a total

Everything outside the cache becomes a visible, named, un-capped block. The cap keeps its four values; it just gets a heading that says what it governs.

C / NO CAP — DEFEND THE PHONE INSTEAD

The real constraint isn't 2 GB; it's the phone filling up

The 11 Pro has filled twice during this project. A ceiling of 2 GB is useless on a phone with 40 GB free and still too much on one with 800 MB. So set the rule in terms of the thing that actually hurts.
