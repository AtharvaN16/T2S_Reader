—— / WHY THE TOTAL BEATS THE DOWNLOAD

The download is the recipe; the plans are the meal prepped for your kitchen

Core ML can't run the downloaded model directly. On first warm-up it compiles the graph into a schedule for the exact chip in this phone — which stage runs on the Neural Engine, which on the GPU, how tensors are laid out. That artefact is the compute plan. It is built locally, never downloaded, it is keyed to the install (every update warms a fresh generation), and on the CPU path it can be the larger half. It is also why a background Prepare refuses to run until a foreground warm-up has happened: building plans is about a minute of a core per stage, and iOS kills background processes for exactly that.

C / TWO THINGS, TWO PRICES

Split the halves — because one of them is nearly free to give back

Today a reader who needs 500 MB has exactly one option: delete the voice and pay 620 MB of Wi-Fi to undo it. But 561.6 MB of what's there rebuilds itself the next time they plug in, at no network cost, and KokoroPlanCache.wipe is already written.
