02

Grid · in the words

Four ways to put the number in the text block under the book. The thing to watch is the left edge of the row: titles run one line or two, so anything placed below the title lands at a different height in every cell, and the row stops reading as a shelf.

03

Grid · on the book

Four that never touch the text block at all. Every one of these costs exactly zero vertical space — the tile stays the height it is today — and none of them can go ragged, because the cover is a fixed 120 pt in every cell. The question is whether a number on the artwork still reads as a shelf of books.

What I still need from you

  • Untouched books. With times gone there is nothing left to say for a book at 0% — "0%" is noise. Trap A in section 04 shows the three ways out: let the line collapse (ragged), hold the space empty (aligned, one dead line), or write something. My pick is to hold the space.
  • Finished books. Green check and "Finished", or just "100%"? Shown as the check.
  • Does the grid need the number at all, or is the fill on the cover enough there, with the exact percentage living in the list and in Details? That would be the cleanest grid by some distance.
