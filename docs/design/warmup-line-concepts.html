The one that matters

A failed warm-up shows a green “Voice ready”. KokoroStatusModel.update calls beginReadyBeat() on any transition out of a warming state (KokoroComposition.swift:154), and .unavailable and .removed both have isWarming == false. So a download that failed, a stage load that failed twice, and a model the reader deleted in Storage all end on the green light and the words “Voice ready”. Then the glow fades and the book reads in the system voice with nothing said.

05 / CYCLING

One thing at a time

Four ways to move between messages, running live. The rule matters more than the choice: cycle only when the wait is long enough to earn it. A 6-second warm-up shows one line and stops — a swap at 3.2 s would be a flicker. Gate it on an expected wait over roughly 12 s, and always land back on the estimate, never mid-rotation, when the green arrives.

07 / IF IT WERE MINE

What I’d build

  • Geometry: 100 × 4. Slight, and it survives the thicker fill without reading as a download bar.
  • Bar: P1 + P4 — the capsule, with the soft leading edge. One radial gradient and a 1.6 s opacity loop, and it fixes the real problem: on a minutes-long A13 warm-up the bar barely moves, and a still bar reads as a hang.
  • Cycling: C2, anchor + rotator. The anchor never moves, so the line never jumps width and never re-reads as a new message; only the half after the “·” crossfades. Gate on an expected wait over 12 s.
  • Copy: ship the fix rows first — they are correctness, not polish. The green “Voice ready” over a failed warm-up is the one a reader can actually be misled by.
  • Then: the cloud-voice anchor. It is the only state where the reader is looking at a pulsing light over a book that is already reading aloud, and today the screen does not explain itself.

Open questions: does the rotator earn its complexity, or is one well-chosen line better? · Should “Waiting for Wi-Fi” become tappable, breaking allowsHitTesting(false)? · Is P6 too clever for a state that also has to work at 6 seconds?
