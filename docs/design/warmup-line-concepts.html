01 / THE LINE

Built, and running

The full first-launch sequence on a 24-second loop: waiting → downloading → the S3 handoff → preparing → warming → ready. The title states the phase and holds still. The subtext cycles beneath it, with the clock sitting after it on the same row — so the bar underneath is alone and perfectly centred. Megabytes appear only while the download is actually running.

One mechanic this needs: the cycling half of that row is measured once per phase to its widest string, so the separator and the clock beside it stay put while the copy changes. Without that the clock would slide left and right on every swap, which is the thing pinning it was meant to stop.

02 / TITLE WEIGHT

How heavy is “higher weight”

Four combinations of the two variables, frozen on the same moment so they can be compared directly. The thing to watch for: at 600 the title stops being a caption and starts being a heading, which is more presence than a transient status line may want on a page someone is reading. At 500 it separates from the subtext without asserting itself.

03 / SUBTEXT

How faint is faint

Same title throughout, cycling live so you can judge the swap at each weight. Below about 40% the subtext stops being reliably legible on the bright ground under the glow’s brightest breath — and this row now carries both the megabytes and the clock, which are the two things in it a reader might squint at. The clock is set a weight heavier than the copy beside it for the same reason.

One rule the layout needs

The subtext row keeps its height even when it is empty. Otherwise a phase with nothing to say — “Checking the voice”, “Voice ready” — collapses the row and the bar jumps 13 pt up the screen, then back down at the next phase. Reserve the height always; leave it blank when there is nothing worth saying. The separator only exists when there is text on both sides of it.

Still the one that matters

A failed warm-up shows a green “Voice ready”. KokoroStatusModel.update calls beginReadyBeat() on any transition out of a warming state (KokoroComposition.swift:154), and .unavailable and .removed both have isWarming == false. So a download that failed, a stage load that failed twice, and a model the reader deleted in Storage all end on the green light and the words “Voice ready”. Then the glow fades and the book reads in the system voice with nothing said.

09 / TO DECIDE

Three things, then I build it

  • Title weight — Medium 11 or SemiBold 11. My read is Medium: SemiBold turns a transient status into a heading on a page someone is reading. §02 has both at true scale.
  • Subtext — 9.5 pt at 50% is what §01 runs. §03 has 9 / 9.5 / 10 and 40 / 50 / 60% so you can pick the pair.
  • Eight or three — whether the warm-up segment subdivides into its eight real stages or stays a single box. §05 shows both. Three is calmer; eight is what the code actually knows.

Everything else is settled: S3 at the seam · 88 × 5, centred and alone · plain for the download · subtext is the only thing that cycles · clock shares the subtext row · megabytes only while downloading · the subtext row keeps its height when empty. The three fix rows in §04 land with the same change.
